Walk in His Footsteps

Śrīla Prabhupāda emphasized that spiritual character grows through steady daily practice—not mere admiration. Check off today’s three foundational commitments.

1. Śravaṇam & Kīrtanam

Attentive Japa Meditation

Even amid crowded engagements in Surat and New York, Śrīla Prabhupāda’s fingers moved continuously within his bead bag, uttering the Hare Kṛṣṇa mahā-mantra as our sole spiritual medicine.

2. Vedabase Study

Daily Scriptural Reading

Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī instructed him: “If you ever get any money, publish books.” Spend 20 minutes daily in Bhagavad-gītā As It Is, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, or Śrīla Prabhupāda-līlāmṛta at vedabase.io/en/library/spl/1/1/.

3. Sat-saṅga & Sevā

Devotee Association & Service

In New York’s storefront and across 108 global temples, Prabhupāda built a loving family united by cooking prasādam, cleaning the altar, and sharing books with compassion.
