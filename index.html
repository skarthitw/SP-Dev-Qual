Applied Bhakti-Yoga

      
Walk in His Footsteps: Practical Devotional Action Guide

      

        Śrīla Prabhupāda emphasised that Kṛṣṇa consciousness is practical action dovetailed with the Supreme Lord. These four daily disciplines translate his example in the Līlāmṛta into steady practice.
      

      

        

          
01 • Sādhana Foundation

          
Guard the Morning Hours

          

            Even aboard the Jaladuta and in Bowery lofts, Śrīla Prabhupāda rose in the early hours to translate and chant. Anchor your day with attentive japa (sixteen rounds for initiated devotees, or a fixed daily vow) and maṅgala-ārati before worldly correspondence begins.
          

        

        

          
02 • Śravaṇaṁ Kīrtanam

          
Read & Repeat Faithfully

          

            To his San Francisco students asking how to speak without full realisation, Prabhupāda taught śrotavyaḥ kīrtitavyaś ca: hear attentively from Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, reflect thoughtfully, and repeat those exact two lines to others without speculation.
          

        

        

          
03 • Yukta-Vairāgya

          
Dovetail Every Resource

          

            Following Rūpa Gosvāmī, Prabhupāda used dictaphones, printing presses, and modern travel solely in Kṛṣṇa’s service. Audit one skill, profession, or household resource this week and consciously engage it in Vaiṣṇava service.
          

        

        

          
04 • Vaiṣṇava Care

          
Be the Honeybee, Not the Fly

          

            In Calcutta (1971), when disciples complained about hypocrites, Prabhupāda reminded them that the honeybee seeks nectar while the fly seeks sores. Cultivate warmth, hospitality with prasādam, and appreciation for every sincere effort in devotees.

Scholarly Footnotes & Source Attribution

  2.

    3. 
        
  3. 
          Diacritic & Textual Convention: Sanskrit and Bengali terms follow standard International Alphabet of Sanskrit Transliteration (IAST) conventions (such as kīrtana, prasādam, sādhana, bhakti-yoga, and saṅkīrtana), while English prose follows Indian English spelling.
        
    4. 
        
  4. 
          Thematic Taxonomy: The six pillars and four study tracks represent an editorial study framework designed to help devotees systematically contemplate Śrīla Prabhupāda’s internal devotion (bhāva), personal discipline (ācāra), and missionary leadership (pracāra).
